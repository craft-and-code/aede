CREDITS & RELATIONSHIPS

Who plays with whom?

Musicians, composers, producers, groups and members: follow album contributions and artist relationships. For classical music, explore works and their movements too.

LYRICS & DISCOVERY

The stories around the sound.

Add lyrics, biographies, cover art and portraits to your catalog on demand. Compare a fetched discography with your collection to spot missing albums.

SEARCH & QUERY

Ask the right question.

Find a title, lyric or note. Combine years, genres, formats, ratings and musical roles in a query, then export the selection as M3U, CSV or JSON.

YOUR PERSPECTIVE

A personal collection.

Favourites, ratings, tags and Markdown notes stay separate from audio files. Saved collections follow their criteria; listening history keeps track of your plays.

COPYING & INTEGRITY

Preserve. Then take along.

check checks the checksums embedded in FLAC frames and Ogg pages. Identical copies can be read back and compared; after conversion, Aède checks structure and duration.

Lossless sources can be converted. Lossy files are copied without re-encoding.
PLAYBACK & ANALYSIS

Play a file, folder, playlist or local selection. Run an analysis with analyze or import a FlacCompagnon report to see its measurements in track details.

03 / THE DSP

Sound.
Your way.

DSP processes the signal during playback. It adjusts levels, tone and output format; your audio files stay untouched.

  • Track or album normalisation.
  • Bass and treble controls, bypassed when flat.
  • Headroom, peak monitoring and spectrum.
  • Adaptation to the output sample rate and format.

THE CURRENT CHAIN

Each stage has its purpose.

MeasurementsTags · FlacCompagnon · cacheLevelGain · headroomToneGraves · aigusOutputSample rate · format

Consistent volume.

ReplayGain and Opus R128, followed by loudness and true peak measurements from FlacCompagnon when available. Album mode preserves level differences between tracks; track mode suits mixed selections.

Clear controls.

Bass and treble act on the decoded signal, with headroom for gain increases. Active stages, peaks and output guard interventions are shown.

Adapted output.

The signal is resampled when the output requires it. Conversion to an integer format uses dither. The displayed spectrum describes the signal sent to the output.

PREMIUM DSP · IN PLANNING

Take your listening further.

Ideas for progressive development.
Scope, funding and schedule to be defined.

01

Parametric equaliser.

Choose the frequency, gain and width of each filter. Combine bell, shelf, high-pass and low-pass filters, with presets and individual bypass.

02

Device profiles.

Keep different settings for headphones, speakers or an output. Import, export and clearly identify the playback profile.

03

Convolution.

Apply filters from user-supplied impulse responses. A foundation for personalised correction, with controlled latency and transitions.

Headphones & listening comfort
Headphone model correction
Optional profiles, subject to available measurements and data rights.
Crossfeed
Mix a small filtered part of each channel into the other to soften stereo separation on headphones.
True peak limiter & night mode
Control peaks and reduce differences between quiet and loud passages, with explicit settings.
Transitions
Configure crossfades between independent tracks while preserving album transitions.
Setup & acoustics
Per-channel settings
Adjust level, balance, delay and polarity.
Bass management
Prepare routing to a subwoofer with crossover and alignment on a suitable output.
Room correction
An idea guided by measurements, requiring a calibrated microphone and acoustic validation.
Dynamic equalisation
Explore correction that changes with the content or listening level.
Ideas to explore
Stereo width & mid/side
Creative controls with mono compatibility checks.
Speed & pitch
Tools for practice and listening, to be validated on transients.
Binaural spatialisation
A research direction for rendering some multichannel content on headphones.

All Premium features above are proposals. Priorities and availability remain to be confirmed.
