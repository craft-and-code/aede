Audio primitives shared by Aède, Phémios and FlacCompagnon.

No catalogue, account, network, history or application dependency. Decoding, queueing and device output are explicit features. The minimal player applies only volume and format adaptation; it has no normalization, EQ, crossfade or bit-perfect route. Rich applications retain their transport policy.
