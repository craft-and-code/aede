Aède — the heart of the music library.

Milestone M0.6: read folders, extract a catalog of interlinked entities from them, answer questions about it, keep what the user thinks of it, and copy a selection of it out to a player or a card.

The formats a library is made of are parsed here, from their specifications; lofty is the single dependency, and covers the long tail of containers that do not deserve a parser of their own.
