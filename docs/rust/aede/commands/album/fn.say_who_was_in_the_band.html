The band as it stood the year this album came out.

A line-up is a fact about an artist and an album is a fact about a year; this is where the two meet, and it is the whole reason the membership dates are worth fetching. Nothing is stored — the answer is worked out from the source’s dates and the album’s year each time it is shown, so it cannot go stale the way a copy would.

Silent unless it has something to say. An album with no year cannot be placed, a compilation has no album artist to ask about, and an artist nobody has fetched has no dates: none of those is a fault, and a page does not fail because a line could not be filled.
