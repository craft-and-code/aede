Aède — command-line interface.

Milestone M0.6: scan folders, build the catalog, query it, record what the user makes of it, and copy a selection out to a player or a card.

ROLE_COMMANDS 🔒
Where a role means something: the listing narrows to the people who hold one, the page narrows to what that person did in it. Two readings of the same word, both useful, and neither of them makes sense without a person — which is why album and track are not here: there, --artist is the filter, and a role with nobody attached asks nothing.
SAID_ELSEWHERE_COMMANDS 🔒
Commands that act on a store of what somebody else said: another tool’s analyses, and the values fetched from a source. Two stores, one vocabulary — --list says what is held, --forget drops it, --source narrows to one — because a user who learned it on one should not have to learn it again on the other.
